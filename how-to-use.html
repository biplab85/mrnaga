How to use Mr Naga

A cooking paste,
not a dare.

Here is how much to use, where to put it, and what to do when you have used too much.

Start with a quarter teaspoon

Stronger than any
hot sauce you own.

Mr Naga is concentrated. There is very little water and almost no filler in the jar, so it is far stronger by volume than any hot sauce you have used.

The rule

¼tsp

A quarter teaspoon, stirred through, tasted, then adjust. You can always add more. You cannot take it back out.

  1. 01MeasureA quarter teaspoon
  2. 02Stir throughAll the way in
  3. 03TasteGive it a moment
  4. 04AdjustThen go again

For a curry serving four

  1. ½tsp

    Warmth and smoke

    The everyday amount. Heat you notice, flavour you remember.

  2. 1tsp

    People will comment

    A dish that gets talked about at the table.

  3. 2tsp

    A decision you are making on purpose

    Only if everyone eating has signed up for it.

Add it late.

Stir it in during the last few minutes of cooking. Long simmering flattens the smoky top notes that make it worth buying.

Where it goes

Eleven places to
put a spoonful.

Each with the amount to start from. Use them as a floor, not a ceiling.

  • 01¼–½ tsp

    Curries and dal

    Stirred through at the end. Works in anything tomato-based, anything creamy, and especially in dal.

  • 021 tsp

    Fried rice and noodles

    One teaspoon through the wok in the last 30 seconds.

  • 031 tsp / 500g

    Marinades

    Chicken, lamb or prawns. Leave an hour. The oil carries it into the meat.

  • 041 : 6
    The gateway product

    Mayo, aioli or ketchup

    1 part Mr Naga to 6 parts sauce. Naga mayo converts more sceptics than anything else on this list.

  • 051 : 6

    BBQ sauce

    Same ratio. Brush onto ribs or wings in the last five minutes of the grill.

  • 061 tsp / 100g

    Butter

    Folded through softened butter, rolled in baking paper, chilled. Melt over steak, corn, prawns, or a jacket potato.

  • 07½ tsp

    Pasta sauce

    Surprisingly good in creamy and tomato sauces. Into a carbonara or an arrabbiata.

  • 081 spoon

    Soup and ramen

    One spoon at the end of a bowl of ramen. Also: a tin of tomato soup, transformed.

  • 09A scrape

    Eggs

    Through scrambled eggs, or on top of a fried egg on toast.

  • 10A thin spread

    Sandwiches and toasties

    A cheese toastie with Mr Naga is one of the best things you can do on a weeknight.

  • 11In the mince

    Burgers

    Mixed through the mince before you form the patty, or spread on the bun.

Three recipes to start with

From two minutes
to forty.

  1. 01 2 minutes

    Naga mayo

    You need

    • 6 tbsp mayonnaise
    • ½ tsp Mr Naga Original
    • A squeeze of lime

    Method

    1. Mix.
    2. Use on burgers, chips, chicken sandwiches, or as a dip for anything fried.

    Why it works: this is the recipe that converts people.

  2. 02 30 minutes

    Naga butter chicken

    You need

    • Your usual butter chicken
    • ½ tsp Mr Naga Original per two serves

    Method

    1. Make your usual butter chicken.
    2. In the final three minutes, stir in the Mr Naga.
    3. Taste. Add more if you are brave.

    Why it works: the smoke sits underneath the cream and completely changes the dish.

  3. 03 40 minutes

    Naga BBQ wings

    You need

    • 1kg chicken wings
    • 1 tbsp oil, and salt
    • 3 tbsp BBQ sauce
    • 1 tsp Mr Naga Gold

    Method

    1. Toss the wings with the oil and salt. Roast at 220°C for 30 minutes.
    2. Mix the BBQ sauce with the Mr Naga Gold and brush it over the wings.
    3. Return to the oven for 5 to 8 minutes.

    Serve with: naga mayo, because you have already made it.

You have used too much

Here is what
actually works.

The myth

Drink water.

Water does not work. Capsaicin is not water soluble, so you are just moving it around.

  • Dairy Milk, yoghurt, or a spoon of raita.
  • Fat A piece of buttered bread, or a mouthful of rice with ghee.
  • Sugar A spoonful of sugar, held in the mouth.
  • Acid A slice of lemon.
In the dish itself

Add yoghurt, cream, coconut milk, or more of the base ingredient. Acid, lemon or vinegar, also pulls the heat back.

On skin

Wash with soap and oil, not water. And wash your hands before you touch your eyes or anyone else's.
