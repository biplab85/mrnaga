What's in the jar

A flavour bomb,
not a hot sauce.

Mr Naga captures the essence, aroma, smokiness, and spiciness of ghost pepper chili and marries it with ancient indian pickling spices for an unforgettable spicy flavour bomb.

Mr Naga is a concentrated chilli paste with very few fillers. Although it can be used like a very spicy indian Achar, it becomes more fun when you cook with it. Best to add some or a lot near the end of your cooking.

  • Ghost pepper The essence, aroma and smokiness of naga chilli.
  • Ancient pickling spices The Indian achar tradition, married to the heat.
  • Concentrated A paste with very few fillers. A little goes far.
  • Add it late Some, or a lot, near the end of your cooking.

Some ideas on how you can use Mr Naga

It becomes more fun
when you cook with it.

  1. 01

    Add as a spicy heat to your curry, daal, biryani, polau, white rice.

  2. 02

    Mix with condiments ie. ketchup, salsa, mayo or BBQ sauce for a zing.

  3. 03

    Mix with marinades for your meat to get an extra smokey heat.

  4. 04

    Goes surprisingly well with creamy, buttery or tomato based pasta sauces.

  5. 05

    Spice up your fried rice, stirfry, soups, ramen, wings or sandwiches.

About us

Burned a place in
people's hearts.

We're passionate about Food and how it makes us feel. We've lived and travelled all over the world before settling down in Australia. Our mission is to introduce some of the most unique flavors that we grew up with and experienced to Australia and New Zealand.

Mr Naga has burned a place in people's heart all over the world for the past 25+ years. Humbly started by an immigrant from Sylhet, Bangladesh who thought to jar their magical and secret family recipe for everyone to try.

The rest is history — without a single dollar spent on marketing they have grown steadily just based on taste.

Who makes it

Mr Naga, is manufactured by Pasha Foods in UK.

Pasha Food Products is a proud family-run business that has been bringing the heat and flavor to dinner tables and kitchens in UK and all over the world with Mr Naga chilli pickle since 1996. The journey began with a simple mission: to share their love for rich, bold flavors with the world.

Over the years, They've remained committed to using only the finest ingredients, crafting each batch with care and a touch of family tradition.
