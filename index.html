Frequently asked
questions

Still curious? Reach us any time at sales@mrnaga.com.au.

Mr Naga is a famous ghost pepper chilli pickle paste manufactured in the UK. Burning into many hearts from the UK to all over the world — and finally arriving in Australia.

Our chilli pickle paste has been perfected over 25 years, using the very same recipe we started with. Mr Naga captures the smoky aroma and fiery kick of the Naga chilli — also known as Bongo, Bhut Jolokia, Bombay or Ghost pepper — in a jar, truly unlike anything else on the market.

What sets it apart is its versatile use, strong flavour, and an aroma that stays vibrant for months even after the jar is opened.

  • Fiery-food enthusiasts who measure meals by heat, not minutes.
  • Home cooks and chefs wanting a reliable, premium ghost pepper chilli flavour and heat.

Each jar will have an expiry date on it. Typically an unopened jar will have 2 years of shelf life from the manufacture date. An open jar will last months in your fridge, but for best results we suggest using it up within 4 months.

Due to the perishable nature of food products, and for health and safety reasons, we do not accept returns or exchanges on food items once they have been delivered — unless the product is defective or an error occurred with your order.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, past its expiry date or incorrect, contact us within 24–48 hours of delivery at sales@mrnaga.com.au with your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photos of the product and packaging.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 15 business days.
